How it works

1

Tell us what your child loves

Add your child's age and interests. Parkour and Lego? Chess and nature documentaries? Sit down together and pick what they're excited about.

2

Our algorithm picks channels which are both fun and enriching

We suggest age-appropriate YouTube channels based on your child's interests. Content that they'll enjoy, but with guardrails. You can review every one and approve or reject.

3

They get their own feed

Bookmark their page. No login, no password, no account. Just the types of videos you've approved.

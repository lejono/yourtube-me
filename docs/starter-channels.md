# Starter Channels

A curated list of YouTube channels suitable for kids, organised by interest. Every channel here has been checked for clean language and age-appropriate content.

Use this as a starting point when setting up YourTube for the first time. Browse the categories that match your child's interests, then add the channel IDs to YourTube via the admin UI.

**How to add a channel:** In the admin panel (`/admin`), paste the channel ID (the `UCxxxxxxx` string listed below) or a YouTube channel URL, add a name, and click Add.

**Age indicators:** Each channel is marked with a rough age range. These are guidelines, not rules — you know your child better than a table does.

## Science & Engineering

| Channel | Ages | Description |
|---------|------|-------------|

| **ElectroBOOM** | 10+ | Iranian-Canadian electrical engineer who gets zapped constantly while explaining circuits. Educational slapstick. Genuinely teaches electronics through comedy. |

| **SciShow** | 10+ | Quick-hit science Q&A. Why do we yawn? How do black holes form? Hank Green and team cover biology, chemistry, physics in 5-10 minute episodes. |

| **The DadLab** | 5+ | Hands-on science experiments using everyday household materials. A dad and his kids build, test, and explore. Great for younger scientists. |
| **Crash Course Kids** | 7-10 | Structured science lessons (ecosystems, gravity, engineering) in the CrashCourse animated style. More school-adjacent but well-produced. |
| **SciShow Kids** | 6-9 | Jessi and her robot rat Squeaks answer kids' actual science questions. Simple, friendly, accurate. |

## Notes for parents

**Start small.** Don't add all of these at once. Pick 5-8 channels that match your child's current interests and add more over time as you see what they watch and what they skip.

**Preview first.** Every channel here has been checked for generally clean content, but individual videos can vary. Channels marked with caveats (like "preview first") occasionally have content that some parents might consider borderline. Use the block feature in YourTube's admin panel if a specific video isn't right.

**Ages are rough guides.** A curious 8-year-old might love Veritasium even if they don't understand everything. A 13-year-old might still enjoy SciShow Kids for quick answers. Let your child's engagement guide you, not the age number.

**Mix it up.** The best feed has a mix of pure entertainment (Dude Perfect, How Ridiculous) and channels that teach without trying too hard (Kurzgesagt, OverSimplified, Numberphile). Kids will self-select, but they can't self-select from a list of channels they've never seen.

**This list is a starting point.** It reflects channels that were active and well-regarded as of early 2026. Channels change, new ones emerge. If you find a great channel that should be on this list, contributions are welcome.
